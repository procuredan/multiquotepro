Common Questions

Cleaning Workers' Comp FAQ

Is workers' comp required for cleaning companies?

Nearly every state requires workers' comp once you have employees, including part-time staff. Rules vary by state and employee count.

How is janitorial workers' comp priced?

On payroll, class codes (commercial vs. residential vs. floor care), claims history, and state rates — which differ by carrier, so comparing matters.

Does it cover part-time and high-turnover staff?

Yes — part-time employees generally count toward coverage. Pay-as-you-go billing can help manage fluctuating, high-turnover payroll.

How can I lower my cleaning workers' comp cost?

Accurate class codes, a clean claims record, safety training, and comparing multiple carriers are the biggest levers.
