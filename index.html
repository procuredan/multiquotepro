Common Questions

Workers' comp insurance FAQ

Is workers' comp insurance required?

Nearly every state requires workers' compensation once you have employees — rules vary by state and number of employees. We help you get compliant coverage at a competitive rate.

Why do quotes differ so much between carriers?

Carriers price class codes, industries, and risk differently, and your experience mod affects each quote. That's exactly why comparing multiple carriers matters.

Can you help new businesses or lapsed coverage?

Yes. New ventures, first-time buyers, and lapsed or cancelled policies are all welcome — we work markets that write them.

How much does it cost to compare?

Nothing. Comparing quotes is free, there's no obligation to switch, and your information is never sold or shared.
