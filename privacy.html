Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Procure Media, LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website multiquotepro.com (“Site”) and submit requests for insurance quotes. We operate in the State of Idaho, and this Policy is intended to comply with applicable Idaho and U.S. privacy regulations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you voluntarily submit through our quote request form, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Organization, HOA, or condo association details
  • Property location
  • Number of units
  • Any notes or message you provide
  • UTM and referral tracking parameters
  • IP address and browser information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit for the following purposes:

  • To match you with licensed insurance partners for the purpose of obtaining insurance quotes.
  • To send your quote request to The Partners Group, our licensed insurance partner.
  • To contact you regarding your submission if needed to complete your request.
  • For internal analytics, fraud prevention, and website optimization.

3. How We Share Your Information

By submitting your information, you consent to our sharing of your details with The Partners Group, a licensed insurance provider or broker who may contact you to provide insurance quotes and related services.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share your information with:

  • The Partners Group (licensed insurance partner)
  • Service vendors who help us operate the website (e.g., form processing, hosting, email delivery)
  • Law enforcement or regulators if required by law

4. TCPA Consent

By submitting your phone number, you provide express written consent for The Partners Group to contact you via phone calls or text messages, including through automated dialing systems or prerecorded messages. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and tracking technologies to understand website usage and improve performance. These may include Google Analytics or similar tools.

6. Data Security

We maintain commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights
